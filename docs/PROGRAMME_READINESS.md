# Programme readiness

This is the evidence checklist FaithCine should complete before its next accelerator or startup-programme application. The website should reflect verified progress only.

## Google for Startups Accelerator: Africa

Google currently describes the programme as best suited to growth-stage, revenue-generating African technology startups. Its published criteria emphasize traction, a scalable product with a significant market and defensible growth model, meaningful AI or machine-learning technology, and active participation from technical leadership.

FaithCine already has a clear Nigeria base, mission, product direction, public website, responsible-technology principles, and research pathway. Evidence still needed:

- a live, testable Selah product;
- named founders and technical leadership;
- user, retention, pilot, revenue or other traction evidence;
- a defined market and growth model;
- a concrete AI architecture and evaluation plan;
- a specific technical challenge for accelerator mentors.

Official programme page: <https://startup.google.com/programs/accelerator/africa/>

## Anthropic Startup Program

Anthropic's published terms say evaluation may consider business traction, investment and funding, along with Claude integration and usage. Benefits may include API credits, priority rate limits and programme resources.

Evidence still needed:

- a real Claude-powered product workflow rather than a proposed integration;
- API usage and evaluation results;
- a clear safety and human-review design;
- pilot usage and business traction;
- investment or funding information where applicable.

Official terms: <https://www.anthropic.com/startup-program-official-terms>

## Application evidence pack

Maintain one verified folder containing:

1. Founder and team biographies with roles and LinkedIn profiles.
2. A one-sentence problem, solution and target user.
3. A product demo and architecture diagram.
4. Monthly active users, retention, pilots, revenue and growth, clearly dated.
5. Research findings and customer quotations with permission.
6. AI model choice, evaluation method, failure cases, safety controls and cost per session.
7. Market size, distribution plan and business model.
8. Incorporation, cap table, funding and data-protection documents.
9. The technical challenge the programme should help solve.

Do not publish or submit estimated traction as achieved traction. Label forecasts, targets and plans clearly.
